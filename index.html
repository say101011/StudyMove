Privacy Policy & Data Processing

App Name: Focus Timer | Effective Date: January 1, 2025

The Focus Timer application ("the App") values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled and processed when you use our App.

1. Data Collection & Processing

The App can be used without registering for an account. We do not collect or store any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) on external servers.

  • Local Storage Only: All user data generated during the use of the App—including timer preferences, focus/workout session logs, and statistical history—is stored exclusively on your device's local storage (Room Database / DataStore Preferences).
  • No Registration: We do not require or collect account credentials, names, phone numbers, or email addresses for using the App.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To support app development and provide core features free of charge, the App integrates Google AdMob (Google LLC) for serving advertisements. Third-party SDKs may automatically collect or process certain technical data, including:

  • Collected Information: Advertising ID (AAID/IDFA), device information (model, OS version), network connectivity status, IP address, and ad interaction/performance metrics.
  • Purpose: Delivering advertisements, providing personalized ads (where consent is given), detecting ad fraud, and measuring performance.
  • Third-Party Privacy Policy: For more information on how Google processes data, please visit Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Data Retention & Destruction

  • Local Data Removal: When you uninstall the App or clear the App's data via your device settings, all locally stored timers, history, and settings are permanently erased from your device.
  • Server Data: Since we do not operate external app servers, no user data is retained or stored by us externally.

4. User Rights & Opt-Out Options

You can manage your privacy and advertising preferences at any time through your device settings:

  • Opting Out of Personalized Ads (Android): Go to [Settings] → [Privacy] or [Google] → [Ads] → [Delete advertising ID] or [Reset advertising ID].
  • Deleting App Data: Uninstall the App or navigate to [Settings] → [Apps] → [Focus Timer] → [Storage] → [Clear Data].

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 14 depending on jurisdiction). It is designed to be safe for all users without account registration.
